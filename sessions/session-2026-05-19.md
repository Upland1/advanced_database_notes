## What I understood
- A KPI is not only a SQL query; it requires a precise business definition, filtering logic, and clear interpretation rules.
- Different aggregation strategies can completely change the meaning of a dashboard metric, especially when dealing with completed, cancelled, overdue, or inactive work.
- CASE expressions, interval arithmetic, and analytical functions are essential for building realistic operational dashboards and severity-based reporting.
- Simple metrics such as raw task counts can become misleading if they do not account for priority, complexity, workload distribution, or completion status.
