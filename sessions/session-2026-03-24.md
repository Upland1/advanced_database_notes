## What I understood
- BEFORE triggers allow modifying or blocking operations before execution
- `:NEW` and `:OLD` provide access to row values during DML operations
- Triggers can automate audit fields like timestamps and users
- Database-level logic can enforce restrictions on UPDATE and DELETE operations
- Custom errors can be raised to prevent invalid actions
- Triggers can validate conditions involving other tables
